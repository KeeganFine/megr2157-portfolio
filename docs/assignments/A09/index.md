## Design Process


### Belt selection
In order to choose the correct size belt, the first thing I did was list out the givens specifications and a drew a quick sketch of the belt and pully diagram. This allowed me to create an idea of what the overall design would look like. The material I chose to use for the pully is aluminum which has a friction coefficient of .3 with a chosen safety factor of 3. Next, I listed the knowns and unknowns of the belt, with this information I created a free body diagram of the belt and pully relationship. To select the correct belt, I needed to know the belt diameter and outer circumference. To find these I used normal stress and yield strength equations factoring in my safety factor. After finding the diameter of the belt I plugged that value into the cross-sectional area equation to find its cross section. The outer circumference was found by plugging in the pully diameter and length into circumference equation. This led me to choose the rubber Round Belt 3044K605 from McMaster-Carr with a diameter of 3/16" and a circumference of 21.5".
